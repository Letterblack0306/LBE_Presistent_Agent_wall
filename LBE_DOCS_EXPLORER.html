Workspace policy & agent continuation — user-directed requirement

Purpose: workspace consistency, not a product veto User requests determine product evolution. Policies preserve the workspace and bound specific effects. Advisory/missing-document checks give actionable information and must not arbitrarily end a task. A denial of one operation is not completion or abandonment of the original task.
Decision categories and required response INFO / WARN: evidence, missing checks or documentation; continue independent work. ACTION_DENIED: preserve the enforced boundary for that operation and explain the exact policy/reason. USER_DECISION_REQUIRED: show the precise user-controlled policy change. HARD_BOUNDARY: never bypass security or authorization; continue only permitted investigation.
Every denied or escalated operation must explain Operation and path; reason code; active policy file/rule and effective value; why it applies; required evidence/checks/documentation; agent-authorized recovery options; whether explicit user approval is required; exact recheck condition; and what work may continue now. Never return an unexplained “BLOCKED” as the entire task verdict.
Changing a user-controlled policy Do not silently edit policy or pretend the user authorized new authority. If the requested feature genuinely conflicts, present CURRENT value → PROPOSED value, exactly which operation/files/scope change, effects, preserved restrictions, duration (task-only or persistent), and what happens after approval or rejection. Apply only the approved delta, record the approval/source, recheck and resume the original task without lost context. Ordinary development within existing authority needs no extra permission ceremony.
Required acceptance — not yet end-to-end proven Test an advisory warning, recoverable missing evidence, scoped policy denial, explicit policy-change approval, refusal of an unapproved change, replay of the exact approved delta, and agent recovery/continuation. Confirm receipts and actual effects. Documentation and green unit tests alone cannot certify this workflow.
