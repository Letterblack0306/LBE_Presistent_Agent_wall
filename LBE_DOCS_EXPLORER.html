Decision categories and required response INFO / WARN: evidence, missing checks or documentation; continue independent work. ACTION_DENIED: preserve the enforced boundary for that operation and explain the exact policy/reason. USER_DECISION_REQUIRED: show the precise user-controlled policy change. Every denied or escalated operation must explain Operation and path; reason code; active policy file/rule and effective value; why it applies; required evidence/checks/documentation; agent-authorized recovery options; whether explicit user approval is required; exact recheck condition; and what work may continue now. Changing a user-controlled policy Do not silently edit policy or pretend the user authorized new authority. If the requested feature genuinely conflicts, present CURRENT value → PROPOSED value, exactly which operation/files/scope change, effects, preserved restrictions, duration (task-only or persistent), and what happens after approval or rejection. Apply only the approved delta, record the approval/source, recheck and resume the original task without lost context. Ordinary development within existing authority needs no extra permission ceremony.
